# /wiki-init

Scaffolds the wiki root structure, configures `wiki_sources.json` using Guided Discovery, and establishes initial workspace settings.

Runs `scripts/init_vault.py` under the hood. For detailed specifications and the full execution protocol, see `skills/obsidian-init/SKILL.md`.
