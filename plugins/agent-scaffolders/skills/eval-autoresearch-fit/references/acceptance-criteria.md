# Acceptance Criteria: eval-autoresearch-fit

**Purpose**: Assess a skill's viability for the Karpathy 3-File Autoresearch autonomous optimization loop.

## 1. Viability Scoring
- **[PASSED]**: The skill is objectively scored on all four dimensions, producing a total viability score out of 40.
- **[FAILED]**: Scoring is subjective, lacks dimension breakdowns, or fails to define optimization loops.

## 2. Inventory Maintenance
- **[PASSED]**: Proposes 3-file architectures and successfully updates the ranked skills inventory.
- **[FAILED]**: Updates to `summary-ranked-skills.json` fail or are skipped.
