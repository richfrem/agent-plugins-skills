## Deterministic findings

The bundled authoring contract owns severity and rule IDs. Errors block structural
readiness: invalid metadata, unresolved required links, malformed evaluations and
representation-specific packaging failures. Warnings identify size, navigation,
compatibility and discoverability gaps. Missing evaluations are reported honestly.
A warning-only pass is not proof of full alignment or behavior.

For human prioritization, P0 is an unusable/unsafe instruction or artifact; P1 is a
likely behavior or portability failure; P2 is a navigation or clarity weakness; P3
is an optional refinement. These priorities do not overwrite deterministic severity.

## Language and preservation review

Check instructions against the requested outcome, source commands, routing boundaries
and evaluation cases. Propose bounded edits supported by evidence. Flag repetition,
excess basics, conflicting terminology, weak examples and unnecessary historical or
conversation retelling. Preserve necessary rationale and mandatory constraints.
Model findings carry confidence and preservation risk; do not treat them as errors
produced by the structural checker. Do not authorize deletion through a review finding.

## Progressive disclosure

SKILL.md is a concise entry point with a starting action, essential constraints,
verification and directly linked reading conditions. Move substantial detail into
focused references, each with early contents when over 100 lines. Keep scripts for
deterministic operations, model instructions for reasoning and references for knowledge.
Instructional skills do not require a script. The local 80-line target is advisory;
Anthropic's under-500-body-line recommendation is separate.
