# Step 6.5: Orchestrator Meta-Survey Protocol

The Orchestrator Meta-Survey is a special retrospective step executed by the ORCHESTRATOR at the end of a multi-session trajectory or loop sequence. Its goal is to analyze the structural health of the OS itself, rather than the target skill under test.

## Key Inquiry Areas

1. **Systemic Bottlenecks**:
   - Are there recurring friction events that point to tooling or protocol ambiguity?
   - Do certain coordination topologies (e.g., Turn-Signal vs. Fan-Out) consistently produce higher error rates?

2. **Tamper & Divergence Detection**:
   - Did any agent attempt to bypass the evaluation gate or write files without triggering events?
   - Is there evidence of semantic drift or map debt accumulating without resolution?

3. **Self-Evolution Triggers**:
   - Has the loop successfully updated its own instructions or scripts?
   - What new rules should be promoted to `AGENTS.md` or `GEMINI.md`?
